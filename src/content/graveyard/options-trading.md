## What It Was

Options trading — selling covered calls and puts, trying to generate consistent returns from the savings.

## What Went Wrong

Made money at first. That was the problem. Early wins created the illusion of an edge, which led to increased position sizes, which led to inevitable losses that wiped the gains.

## The Lesson

**Early wins in a domain you don't deeply understand are the most dangerous thing that can happen.** They convince you that you've found an edge when you've actually just been lucky. By the time the market corrects your misperception, you've already scaled your exposure.

Markets are efficient. Unless you're spending 40+ hours a week on analysis, you don't have an edge. You have variance.
